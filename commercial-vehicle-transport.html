BEFORE YOU CALL

Common trade questions.

How many cars can your car transporter carry?

Our two-deck car transporter takes up to six cars at once. For bigger moves, tell us how many vehicles you have and we’ll plan the loads.

How much does car transport cost in Ireland?

It depends on how many cars you’re moving, the distance and whether they all run. Moving several cars on one load brings the cost per car down. Send us the details for a free trade quote.

Do you collect imported cars from Dublin Port?

Yes. We collect imported and newly arrived vehicles from Dublin Port and deliver them to your premises, a storage compound or a prep centre.

Can you move cars that don’t run?

Yes. Non-runners, damaged and high-value cars can go on our flatbed. Let us know when you send the job details.

Do you work outside Dublin?

Yes. We deliver throughout Ireland.

Can you do regular runs, not just one-off jobs?

Yes. Tell us your typical volumes and schedule and we’ll quote for one-off or recurring work.

How do we get a quote?

Use the form on this page, email info@ottrecoverybreakdown.ie or call 086 195 2598 with the number of vehicles, collection and delivery points and your dates.
