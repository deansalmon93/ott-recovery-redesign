SAFETY WHILE YOU WAIT
  • Pull in as far from traffic as you safely can.
  • Apply the handbrake, switch off the engine and turn on your hazard lights.
  • Leave the car by the passenger side, away from oncoming traffic.
  • Wear a reflective jacket and set out a warning triangle if you have them.
  • Wait somewhere safe behind a barrier if you can, then call us.

BEFORE YOU CALL

A few useful answers.

How do I know if I need a new battery?

Slow cranking, warning lights or a battery that keeps going flat after a jump start are common signs. Our team can advise.

Can you replace the battery where the car is?

Call us with your vehicle details and location and we’ll tell you the best option — a roadside replacement or recovery to a garage.
