DO YOU NEED A JUMP START?

Back on the road

Flat batteries are one of the most common reasons a car won’t start — often from leaving the lights or radio on. Call us and we’ll send our team to jump start your car wherever it is in Dublin and the surrounding counties.

  • 24/7 jump starts at home, at work or at the roadside
  • Advice on whether your battery needs replacing

SAFETY WHILE YOU WAIT
  • Pull in as far from traffic as you safely can.
  • Apply the handbrake, switch off the engine and turn on your hazard lights.
  • Leave the car by the passenger side, away from oncoming traffic.
  • Wear a reflective jacket and set out a warning triangle if you have them.
  • Wait somewhere safe behind a barrier if you can, then call us.

BEFORE YOU CALL

A few useful answers.

Why does my battery keep going flat?

Lights or the radio left on are common causes, but an old battery or a charging fault can do it too. Our team can advise when we get you started.

Can you jump start a van?

Yes. We help with cars and vans.

What if a jump start doesn’t work?

We can look at a battery replacement or recover the vehicle to a garage or your home.
