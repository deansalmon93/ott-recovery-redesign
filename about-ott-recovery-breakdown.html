WHO WE ARE

Local people
getting you home.

OTT Recovery Breakdown is based in Rush, Co. Dublin. We help people across Dublin and the surrounding counties with all kinds of vehicle trouble — accidents, flat tyres with no spare, clutch failures and everything in between.

A breakdown on a busy road or motorway can be dangerous. That’s why we respond as quickly as we can and get your vehicle off the road, whether that means fixing it on the spot or recovering it to safety.

Contact our team for a free quote.
