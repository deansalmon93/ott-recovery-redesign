VEHICLE ACCIDENT RECOVERY

First, make sure
everyone is safe.

If anyone is hurt, call the emergency services on 112 or 999 first. When it’s safe, call us: we’ll send a recovery vehicle to collect your car or van and take it to the destination you choose.

  • Recovery from the roadside after a collision
  • Up to seven passengers transported with the vehicle
  • Taken to a garage, bodyshop, home or other destination
  • Cars, vans and larger vehicles
EXPERTS IN ACCIDENT RECOVERY

Careful handling
of damaged vehicles.

We’ve served Dublin and the surrounding counties for over 20 years. Whatever the crash or damage, we make sure your vehicle gets the right treatment and is moved without causing further harm.

We’re open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call for a quick, free quote.

SAFETY WHILE YOU WAIT
  • Pull in as far from traffic as you safely can.
  • Apply the handbrake, switch off the engine and turn on your hazard lights.
  • Leave the car by the passenger side, away from oncoming traffic.
  • Wear a reflective jacket and set out a warning triangle if you have them.
  • Wait somewhere safe behind a barrier if you can, then call us.

BEFORE YOU CALL

A few useful answers.

Should I call you or the emergency services first?

If anyone is injured or the road is blocked, call 112 or 999 first. Then call us to recover your vehicle.

Can you take my car to a specific garage or bodyshop?

Yes. Tell us where you’d like it taken when you call.

Can my passengers travel with you?

Yes, our trucks carry up to seven passengers.
