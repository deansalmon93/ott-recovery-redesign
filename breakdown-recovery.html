SAFETY WHILE YOU WAIT
  • Pull in as far from traffic as you safely can.
  • Apply the handbrake, switch off the engine and turn on your hazard lights.
  • Leave the car by the passenger side, away from oncoming traffic.
  • Wear a reflective jacket and set out a warning triangle if you have them.
  • Wait somewhere safe behind a barrier if you can, then call us.

BEFORE YOU CALL

A few useful answers.

Where will you take my vehicle?

Wherever you need it — a garage, your home, or another destination in Ireland. Tell us when you call.

Can my passengers come too?

Yes. Our trucks can carry up to seven passengers along with your vehicle.

Do you recover vehicles from motorways?

Yes, including the M50. Stay safely behind the barrier if you can and call us with your nearest junction.
