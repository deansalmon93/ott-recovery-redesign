CAR WRONG FUEL REPLACEMENT

It happens more
than you’d think.

Putting the wrong fuel in is common, especially when you’re in a rush or driving a new or unfamiliar car. It only takes picking up the wrong nozzle without thinking.

  • Don’t start the engine — or switch it off straight away if you have
  • Don’t turn the ignition on
  • Put the car in neutral and move it somewhere safe if you need to
  • Call us with your location and the fuel you used
WRONG FUEL, BIG MISTAKE

Act before
the damage spreads.

If the engine has been running, wrong petrol or diesel can flood the engine and you’ll soon break down. Acting quickly keeps the repair simpler and cheaper.

We’re open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call for a quick, free quote — competitive prices and quality work.

BEFORE YOU CALL

A few useful answers.

I’ve already started the engine. What now?

Switch it off as soon as it’s safe, don’t restart it, and call us.

Can you help at the petrol station?

Yes. Tell the station staff, keep the car off and call us with the station’s location.

Is it petrol in diesel or diesel in petrol — does it matter?

Both need sorting before you drive. Tell us which fuel went in and roughly how much.
